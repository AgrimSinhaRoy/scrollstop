Screen Time Types

Not all screens
are equal

● Passive Mindless consumption

You watch, scroll, and absorb — but your brain isn't building anything. The algorithm picks the next dopamine hit. You don't even realise an hour has passed.

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
Binge-watching Netflix / YouTube
Infinite feed scrolling
Autoplay with no intent
↓ Every extra hour of passive screen time reduces FSI by 8–12 points on average.
● Active Engaged creation

You choose what to make, play, or learn. Your brain is in the loop — problem-solving, deciding, building. Time is intentional, not stolen.

Coding, drawing, photo/video editing
Intentional gaming with strategy
Video calls with friends
Researching or learning new skills
↑ Active screen use preserves and builds the same attention networks passive scrolling erodes.
